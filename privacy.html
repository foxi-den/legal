Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

TV Launcher is a private, non-commercial project that runs on one household TV. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests one read-only permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. With it the app reads: the name and picture of the signed-in YouTube channel, the list of channels the person subscribes to, and basic details (title, length, live status) of the newest public videos of those channels.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display "new from your channels" and "live now" on the TV home screen. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored in a file on the TV itself. The subscription and video information is kept in the memory of a small service on the same TV and, for speed, in the TV's local browser storage. TV Launcher has no cloud server and no database of its own, and nothing is uploaded anywhere except the requests the TV sends directly to Google and YouTube.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TV Launcher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing the sign-in file from the TV (or resetting the app) deletes all stored data. There is nothing stored elsewhere.

YouTube Terms of Service

By using the YouTube features, users are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
