TV Launcher

TV Launcher is a personal, non-commercial home screen for a living-room TV. It shows the owner's own PlayStation, Plex and YouTube information on one screen.

For YouTube, the app signs in with Google's device sign-in and reads, with read-only access (youtube.readonly), the channels the signed-in person is subscribed to and the newest public videos of those channels, so the home screen can show "new from your channels". It never posts, edits, likes, comments or deletes anything.
